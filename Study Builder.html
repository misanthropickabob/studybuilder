Study Builder

This is not an official University of Arizona or course sanctioned study tool. Use at your own risk. This tool was made by a fellow student to help organize information and make it easier to study. As per the syllabus, it is for your own personal use only: do not post any content created with this builder on any other website. You must regularly export to Google Sheets to save your work unless you are working within your own Claude account.

Paste each week's learning objectives and key terms, write your own questions, or use the University's AI with your textbook screenshots to draft questions for you to review. The builder turns them into an objective-first study guide, a key-term sheet you define in your own words, spaced-repetition flashcards, a due-date checklist and a day-by-day plan to your next exam.

      One card per line as question | answer | objective | source | kind. Only question and answer are required. Objective: 5 for the fifth module objective, D2 for the second discussion objective. Source: the page or section the answer comes from, so you can reread it (for example p. 391). Kind: recall, understanding or application; left blank, the page guesses from the wording. Aim for a mix: about a third each. The Objectives tab then shows which objectives have no cards yet. Terms you define on the Key terms tab become cards too.
      Paste the graded-assessments and due-dates part of the weekly announcement as-is. The page finds the dates, matches them to the assignments, and gives undated quizzes the module-close date. You can also type one per line as name | due date.

      Your work is saved in this browser only. To keep it, export it to a Google Sheet: the button copies every week as rows, then you paste into a sheet. To continue later on any device, copy the rows out of the sheet and restore them here.

      Open a new Google Sheet
      1. Rows copied. Open a new Google Sheet (link above).
      2. Click cell A1 and paste (Ctrl+V or ⌘+V). Each week's objectives (with your rephrasings), terms, definitions, flashcards and check marks land in columns.
      3. Name the sheet after your course. Export again whenever you have added work; pasting over A1 replaces the old rows.
      Restore from a sheet

      In your sheet, select all the rows (including the header row), copy, paste them below, then press Restore. This replaces what is in the builder now.
